Accessibility

Accessibility statement

AliTechGrid Canada aims to provide a clear and usable online experience.

The website is designed with keyboard navigation, visible focus behaviour, semantic headings, readable contrast, responsive layouts and descriptive alternative text.

Need assistance?

If you experience difficulty using the website or booking page, email contact@alitechgrid.com and describe the page or task. We will make a reasonable effort to provide the information or service in another accessible format.

Third-party booking content

The embedded appointment interface is supplied by Zoho. Accessibility issues inside that embedded interface may depend on Zoho's implementation. Customers may alternatively request assistance by email.
