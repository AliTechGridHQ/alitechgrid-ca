What AI-enabled means

Clear language instead of exaggerated claims

AI-assisted website: AI helps prepare draft wording, page structure, FAQs and metadata, with human review before publication.

Smart website: approved FAQ responses, booking, contact routing, analytics and automated navigation operate without an ongoing AI-model charge.

Generative-AI website: a secured backend connects the website to a language model. Usage limits, privacy controls and ongoing management are required and are quoted separately.

Client ownership and safety

  • The client should own the domain, business email, phone number and customer data.
  • AliTechGrid receives only the access needed to complete and support the work.
  • API keys and passwords are never placed in public website code.
  • Payment-card information and confidential documents are handled through established third-party services, not ordinary website forms.
  • No website is described as completely secure, unhackable or guaranteed to rank first in search results.
