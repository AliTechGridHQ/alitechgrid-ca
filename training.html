Straight answers

Before you book.

Do I need previous IT experience?

Not for foundation workshops unless a prerequisite is clearly stated. We confirm your current level before recommending the workshop and duration.

Is the 8-hour workshop one long day?

It can be delivered as one practical day or split into shorter sessions when scheduling and workshop design allow. The total training time and delivery plan are confirmed before booking.

Do I receive a certificate?

Yes. Eligible workshops include an AliTechGrid Canada Certificate of Completion. It confirms completion of that workshop; it is not an industry certification, vendor credential or licence.

Is employment or an internship guaranteed after training?

No. Training and employment are separate. From time to time, selected participants who demonstrate strong practical skills may be invited to apply for limited paid junior technical work when suitable business needs arise. No placement, minimum hours or employment is guaranteed.

Can my company request a custom workshop?

Yes. Tell us the technology, staff level, objective and preferred delivery approach. We will confirm a practical scope, duration, schedule and price before booking.

What data is used in cybersecurity, AI and troubleshooting labs?

Training uses authorized lab environments and training, synthetic, public or otherwise approved data. Participants should not enter confidential employer, customer or personal information into external tools unless specifically authorized.

How do pricing, cancellation and refunds work?

Final price, taxes where applicable, schedule, location, prerequisites, equipment requirements, cancellation terms and refund terms are disclosed before payment. We do not ask you to commit before the training scope is clear.
