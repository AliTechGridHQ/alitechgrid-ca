Before you book

Straight answers.

Are these full professional certifications?

No. AliTechGrid offers short-form, practical skills training and an AliTechGrid Certificate of Completion where eligible. It is not a vendor certification, licence or post-secondary credential.

Why are different roles different lengths?

The advertised hours are based on the size of the core practical skill set. A help-desk pathway can be narrower than a computer-network-technician pathway, so they should not be forced into the same duration.

What if I need more time for a core skill?

Eligible role pathways include the stated complimentary Skills Completion Support for listed core outcomes, subject to the published conditions and scheduling availability.

Is employment with AliTechGrid guaranteed?

No. Training and employment are separate. Selected participants may occasionally be invited to apply for limited paid junior technical work when suitable business needs arise, but no placement, hours or employment is guaranteed.

Can I get an estimate before committing?

Yes. Use the Training Plan & Estimate tool to select a role, format and participant count. It generates a tentative learning plan, estimated fee and payment schedule before final confirmation.

What about data, cybersecurity and AI labs?

Labs use authorized training, synthetic, public or otherwise approved data and environments. Do not enter confidential customer, employer or personal information into external tools unless specifically authorized.
