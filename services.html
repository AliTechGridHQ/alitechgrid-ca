Services

Repair, installation and practical IT support

Focused services for customers who need a working device, a reliable setup and a clear path from diagnosis to completion.

01

Computer & laptop repair

Diagnosis and support for common Windows and computer problems.

  • Startup, slowdown and application problems
  • Operating-system repair or reinstallation assessment
  • SSD, RAM and storage upgrade assessment
  • Battery, charger, keyboard and common component replacement assessment
  • Virus and unwanted-software removal
02

Mobile phones & tablets

Initial diagnosis for common phone and tablet issues. Service depends on device model, parts availability and repair feasibility.

  • Screen and battery assessment
  • Charging and connectivity problems
  • Software setup, reset and migration assistance
  • Protective setup and data-transfer support
03

On-site and pickup/drop-off

Service options are confirmed after booking. Pickup/drop-off and on-site availability depend on location, schedule and the type of work.

  • Home technology troubleshooting
  • Small-business device support
  • Printer and peripheral setup
  • Pickup and secure return by confirmation
04

Wi-Fi, network & installation

Practical setup for reliable home and small-office use.

  • Router and wireless configuration
  • Device connectivity and access troubleshooting
  • Printer, display and peripheral installation
  • New computer and user setup
05

Data transfer and backup assistance

Migration and backup support with clear limits. Data recovery cannot be guaranteed.

  • Old-to-new computer transfer
  • Backup setup and restore assistance
  • Storage organization and migration
  • Data-recovery assessment and referral where appropriate
06

Small-business IT support

Foundational support for businesses that need dependable day-to-day technology.

  • Business email and domain setup
  • Cloud productivity account setup
  • Workstation onboarding and standardization
  • Basic network and device documentation
