About

Technology support built around practical outcomes

AliTechGrid Canada is the Canadian service division of AliTechGrid, focused on repair, installation and accessible IT support for homes and small businesses.

Our purpose

Customers should not have to navigate unnecessary complexity to obtain reliable help. AliTechGrid Canada starts with a clear problem description, confirms the service path, diagnoses the issue and seeks approval before additional work.

Services are designed for real customer needs: computers that will not start, laptops that need upgrades, mobile devices that require assessment, unreliable home Wi-Fi and small businesses that need practical technology support.

Operating principles

  • Clear scope and customer approval
  • Respectful handling of devices and information
  • No guarantee of results that cannot honestly be guaranteed
  • Simple communication and documented next steps
  • Referral when specialist work is outside current capability
Service area

Coquitlam and nearby Greater Vancouver communities

Initial service coverage includes Coquitlam, Port Moody, Burnaby, New Westminster and nearby locations. Exact availability, travel and pickup/drop-off arrangements are confirmed before service.
