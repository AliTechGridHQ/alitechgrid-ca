How estimates work

  1. Submit the device, problem and service location.
  2. AliTechGrid reviews the information and confirms whether an estimate can be provided remotely.
  3. If diagnosis is required, the diagnostic, travel or pickup fee is confirmed before service.
  4. A final quotation is provided for approval before additional labour or parts.
  5. Approved work is completed, tested and invoiced.
